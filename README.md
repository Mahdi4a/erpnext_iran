# erpnext_iran

Iran localization for ERPNext, on top of `iran_base`. It adds custom fields and validation hooks; it
does not change ERPNext.

- Customer and Supplier: National ID (کد ملی), Legal ID (شناسه ملی), Economic Code (کد اقتصادی), checked on save
- Address: postal code checked when the country is Iran
- "Iran VAT" sales and purchase tax templates for every company (rate from `iran_vat_rate`, default 10; check the current legal rate)
- Chart of accounts "Iran - Chart of Accounts" (کدینگ حسابداری), offered on the Company form when the country is Iran. The coding (1 current assets, 2 non-current assets, 3 current liabilities, 4 non-current liabilities, 5 equity, 6 income, 7 costs) is a common convention, not an official standard: review it with your accountant
- IRR and IRT currencies with whole numbers, and Iran defaults for the site (number format `#,###`, no currency decimals, Saturday as first day of the week, Asia/Tehran, Persian as the site language) applied on install only where the stock value is still in place; an Iranian company without a currency gets IRR
- "Use Persian Digits" in ERPNext Iran Settings: Persian digits in lists, reports and read-only values (inputs stay 0-9); `{{ value | persian_digits }}` for print formats
- Jalali (Shamsi) fiscal years named after the Jalali year (1404, 1405...), starting 1 Farvardin: "Create Jalali Fiscal Year" button on the Fiscal Year list, the current and next year are also created daily. ERPNext only accepts a year of exactly one Gregorian year, so a Jalali year of another length gets "Is Short Year" ticked; if a year overlaps an existing one, give it a company
- "Show Jalali Dates" in ERPNext Iran Settings: Date and Datetime values in lists and reports are shown as Jalali (stored and entered as Gregorian); `{{ doc.posting_date | jalali_date }}` for print formats
- Iran Cheque (چک دریافتنی / پرداختنی, Erpnext Iran module, `/desk/iran-cheque`): submittable, status buttons Deposit / Endorse / Mark Cleared / Mark Bounced, each posting a Journal Entry (flow in `erpnext_iran/cheques.py`). Accounts come from ERPNext Iran Settings > Cheque Accounts, else from account numbers 110402, 110401 and 210201 of the Iran chart. Amounts are in the company currency. Sayad ID is only checked for 16 digits
- Amount in words in Persian with the unit after the words ("فقط یک میلیون و دویست و پنجاه هزار ریال.") on every document in Rial or Toman when the user reads Persian; other currencies and languages keep ERPNext's own text
- Bank Account: an Iranian Sheba (IR + 24 digits, with or without IR and spaces) is checked with the ISO 13616 mod 97 test and stored as `IR...`
- Iranian banks (31, with a Sheba Bank Code field on Bank) added on install and migrate; a Bank Account with a Sheba gets its bank filled in, and a warning when the chosen bank has another code. The codes come from the public iran-bank-detector data, not from the Central Bank: check them before relying on them, and add any bank that is missing
- Holiday List "تعطیلات رسمی ایران 1405": every Friday and the 22 official holidays that are not on a Friday, set as the default of an Iranian company that has none. The Jalali-calendar holidays are in the code; the religious ones follow the moon and are typed in `holidays.py` once the official calendar of the year is out. 1405 is from the calendars of asriran.com and nabzebourse.com (they differ on 25 Farvardin, which is left out): check it against the official calendar. A year with no entry, such as 1406, gets no list yet
- Payment modes کارت‌به‌کارت, کارت‌خوان, درگاه پرداخت اینترنتی and پایا و ساتنا (stock modes stay), and the fixed Rial-Toman rate (1 Toman = 10 Rial). Dollar and other market rates are entered by hand in Currency Exchange: the app has no source for them
- Zarinpal (زرین‌پال) as a payment gateway for Payment Request: fill in Zarinpal Settings (Merchant ID, Live Mode) and add a Payment Gateway Account for the gateway "Zarinpal", in Rial or Toman. The customer pays at Zarinpal and comes back to `/api/method/erpnext_iran.zarinpal.callback`, which verifies the payment with the amount that was asked for, once, and tells the Payment Request. Needs the `payments` app (branch version-16) for the Payment Gateway doctype; without it the settings page exists but nothing calls it. ERPNext 16 has no `on_payment_authorized` on a Payment Request, which the gateways of the payments app call, so after a verified payment the app calls `set_as_paid` itself, as the system, because the customer coming back from the bank is a guest The sandbox has to be asked for (Allow Sandbox) outside developer mode. Tried on a local site: the payment request and the sandbox page with the right amount are real; the verify call was replaced by "paid", because a sandbox payment needs the customer to fill the card form in the browser. With that, the Payment Request became Paid, one Payment Entry was made and the invoice was paid, and a second callback changed nothing
- Address: a Province (استان) list of the 31 provinces and a City box that suggests the cities of the chosen province (1,479, from the official country divisions file up to the end of 1404 as published in sajaddp/list-of-cities-in-Iran, GPL-3.0; the box still takes any text). Both are written to State/Province and City, which the address print and every report read
- Dollar Rate in ERPNext Iran Settings: one number, saved as the Currency Exchange rate of today for USD to IRR and USD to IRT (saving the settings again the same day corrects it). There is no automatic source for the rate
- Jalali parts for naming series: `JY` (1405), `JYY` (05), `JM` and `JD`, from the posting or transaction date, e.g. `ACC-SINV-.JY.-.#####` gives `ACC-SINV-1405-00001`. Nothing is switched on its own, because it changes document numbers: the buttons Add Jalali Series and Remove Jalali Series in ERPNext Iran Settings add or take away a Jalali series next to each Gregorian one of the sales, purchase and payment documents and make it the default. A new series counts from 1
- "Iran Sales Invoice" print format (Persian, right to left, Jalali date, buyer's national / legal / economic ids, VAT lines, signature boxes): choose it in the Sales Invoice print view. Digits follow "Use Persian Digits". The layout is not the official tax-authority invoice form
- Persian print formats "Iran Purchase Invoice", "Iran Quotation", "Iran Sales Order", "Iran Delivery Note" and "Iran Payment Entry", with the same right-to-left layout, Jalali dates and amount in words. They are made from one layout in `print_formats.py` and rewritten on every migrate, so change them there, not in the Print Format form

- Moadian (سامانه مؤدیان), data model and draft payload only. **Unverified**: the field names and code lists are from memory of the public description, not from an official document. Custom fields: Sales Invoice (Moadian section: inty, inp, ins, tob, setm, taxid, status), Item (goods or service ID), UOM (unit code), Company (tax memory ID, with Tax ID as the economic code). `erpnext_iran.moadian.get_payload(invoice_name)` returns the draft payload and a list of missing data. There is no tax ID generation, signing, encryption or API call. Assumptions to decide: amounts are whole Rials (IRT x 10), one VAT rate per invoice (first tax row), invoice time is Tehran time as Unix milliseconds
